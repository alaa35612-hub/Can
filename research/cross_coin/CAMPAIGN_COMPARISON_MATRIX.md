## Required comparison procedure

1. Reconstruct each campaign independently against the symbol's own history.
2. Group only after reconstruction.
3. Compare successes with failures and ordinary controls inside each group.
4. Identify necessary evidence, supporting evidence, discriminators and failure filters.
5. Split mixed groups rather than force one taxonomy.
6. Create a new family when repeated sequences are not explained by the existing corpus.
7. Record early detectability separately from final return.

## Prohibition

Do not derive a rule merely because selected files contain top gainers. Sample membership is not causal evidence.
