## Use rules

- Create a full hypothesis card before invoking a pattern in a case study.
- Record supporting, opposing and missing evidence.
- Preserve failed analogues and counterexamples.
- Split a pattern when one label hides materially different causal mechanisms.
- Never promote a family based only on one successful coin or persuasive prior prose.
