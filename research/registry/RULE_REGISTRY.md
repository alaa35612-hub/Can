# Structural Research Rule Registry

| Rule ID | Adaptive statement | Status | Mechanism | Required ordered evidence | Supporting cases | Failed/contradictory cases | Controls | Blind replay | Scope | Exceptions | Last decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Allowed statuses: `BACKGROUND_CONCEPT`, `RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS`, `SUPPORTED_PATTERN`, `CONDITIONAL_RULE`, `DURABLE_RULE`, `REJECTED_RULE`, `DEPRECATED`.

A rule cannot become `DURABLE_RULE` unless it recurs across independent campaigns, distinguishes successes from failures and controls, survives causal blind replay, avoids symbol/outcome fitting, states its regime scope, uses adaptive evidence and exposes invalidation and exceptions.

Legacy numeric thresholds are provenance only. Rewrite operational use as questions about symbol-local abnormality, persistence, execution-value confirmation, price acceptance and regime context.

Decision vocabulary: `PROMOTE`, `RESTRICT`, `KEEP_AS_HYPOTHESIS`, `REJECT`, `DEPRECATE`.
