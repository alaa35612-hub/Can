- `RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS`
- `SUPPORTED_PATTERN`
- `CONDITIONAL_RULE`

- `REJECTED_RULE`
- `DEPRECATED`

## Promotion gate

A rule cannot become `DURABLE_RULE` unless it recurs across independent campaigns, distinguishes successes from failures and controls, survives causal blind replay, avoids symbol/outcome fitting, states its regime scope, uses adaptive evidence and exposes invalidation and exceptions.

## Legacy threshold treatment

Record original numbers for provenance only. Rewrite operational use as questions about symbol-local abnormality, persistence, execution-value confirmation, price acceptance and regime context.

## Decision vocabulary

`PROMOTE`, `RESTRICT`, `KEEP_AS_HYPOTHESIS`, `REJECT`, `DEPRECATE`.
