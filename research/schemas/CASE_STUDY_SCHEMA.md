# Campaign Case Study Schema

Use one case study per distinct campaign, not one per final symbol outcome.

## Required sections

1. Identity, source files, campaign boundaries and data-quality status.
2. Research question and tested mechanism.
3. Causal factual timeline covering price, OI/OI value, execution, positioning, acceptance/rejection, higher-timeframe context and causal availability.
4. Campaign segmentation without forcing every state.
5. Dominant, alternative, failure/noise and unidentified hypotheses.
6. Supporting, opposing and missing evidence; discriminators and invalidation.
7. Adversarial review and strongest failed analogue.
8. Previous-analysis audit where applicable.
9. Cutoff-by-cutoff blind replay with frozen decisions.
10. Successful analogues, failed analogues and ordinary matched controls.
11. Rule implications linked to registry IDs.
12. Current-scan implications separated into structural importance, readiness, entry safety, reliability and research confidence.
13. Final conclusion stating what is known, unresolved and what evidence would change the judgment.

A case study is incomplete if it only explains the move after the outcome or omits counterexamples.
