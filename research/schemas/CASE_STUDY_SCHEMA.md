# Campaign Case Study Schema

Use one case study per distinct campaign, not one per final symbol outcome.

## 1. Identity and sources

- case ID;
- symbol and timeframe context;
- campaign boundaries;
- source files and provenance;
- data-quality status, gaps, duplicates and field coverage.

## 2. Research question

State what mechanism, detectability problem, previous error or candidate rule the case tests.

## 3. Causal factual timeline

For every material timestamp record price structure, OI/OI value, execution, positioning, acceptance/rejection, higher-timeframe context, quality flags and whether the observation was available at the active cutoff.

## 4. Campaign segmentation

Record birth, early build, warning, armed, ignition, acceptance, expansion, continuation/reload, cooling, weakness, failure, distribution, reset or rebuild where supported. Do not force every stage.

## 5. Competing hypotheses

For the dominant, alternative, failure/noise and unidentified hypotheses record:

- prerequisites;
- expected ordered sequence;
- causal mechanism;
- supporting evidence;
- opposing evidence;
- missing evidence;
- discriminators;
- invalidation;
- research status.

## 6. Adversarial review

Document the strongest challenge to the dominant explanation, failed analogues with similar surface features, possible leakage, unidentifiable intent claims and whether the conclusion survives removal of one indicator.

## 7. Previous-analysis audit

When applicable include original conclusion, data available then, evidence misused, evidence omitted, alternatives, leakage assessment, corrected judgment and methodological lesson.

## 8. Blind replay

Include a cutoff-by-cutoff trace showing frozen facts, ledger state, hypotheses, decision, confidence, next discriminator and hidden/visible outcome status.

## 9. Analogues and controls

- successful analogues;
- failed analogues;
- ordinary matched control windows;
- material similarities and differences;
- symbol-specific behavior.

## 10. Rule implications

List rules supported, restricted, rejected, deprecated or newly proposed. Link every claim to registry IDs and state limitations.

## 11. Current-scan implication

Explain only evidence-backed implications for detecting partial sequences today. Separate structural importance, readiness, entry safety, reliability and confidence.

## 12. Final research conclusion

State what is known, what remains unresolved, what evidence would change the conclusion, and why this case improves or fails to improve early detection.
