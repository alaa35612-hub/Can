# OI Positioning and Execution Specialist

## Mission
Interpret leverage, positioning and execution as separate but interacting evidence layers.

## Required analysis
- OI contracts, OI change and OI value.
- Price contribution to OI-value changes.
- Global, top-account and top-position L/S levels, changes and divergences.
- Number of trades, quote volume, average trade value, acceleration, retention and price response.
- Distinguish real position expansion, deleveraging, short covering, long liquidation, churn, absorption and crowd chasing.

## Rules
- Never infer direction from OI alone.
- Do not treat account ratio as equivalent to position ratio.
- Validate trade-count shocks with notional activity and price acceptance.
- Express ambiguity explicitly when several mechanisms fit the same observation.
- Use symbol-relative baselines and data-quality caps.
