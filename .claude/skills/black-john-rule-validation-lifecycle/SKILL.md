# Black John Rule Validation Lifecycle

## Mission
Control how observations become hypotheses and how hypotheses may become durable research rules.

## Rule statuses
- `BACKGROUND_CONCEPT`: interpretive idea only.
- `RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS`: unvalidated candidate explanation.
- `SUPPORTED_PATTERN`: supported by several cases but not fully controlled.
- `CONDITIONAL_RULE`: valid only within documented contexts.
- `DURABLE_RULE`: survived diverse cases, controls and blind replay.
- `REJECTED_RULE`: failed discriminative testing.
- `DEPRECATED`: replaced or shown to cause recurrent errors.

## Rule registry fields
- rule ID and version;
- statement in adaptive language;
- causal mechanism;
- applicable symbols, timeframes, liquidity and regimes;
- required ordered evidence;
- supporting cases;
- failed and contradictory cases;
- matched controls;
- false-positive and false-negative analysis;
- blind-replay results;
- exceptions and overrides;
- confidence and current status;
- provenance and revision history.

## Promotion requirements for DURABLE_RULE
A candidate must:
- recur across independent symbols or campaigns;
- explain successful cases;
- discriminate successful cases from failures and controls;
- survive strict historical cutoffs;
- avoid symbol-name or outcome-specific fitting;
- remain valid across documented regimes or be explicitly conditional;
- use symbol/context-adaptive evidence rather than universal analytical thresholds;
- expose failure conditions and known exceptions.

## Threshold conversion
Legacy numeric thresholds are priors for investigation, not gates. Rewrite them as relative questions about symbol-local historical abnormality, persistence, cross-feature confirmation and regime context. Preserve original values only as provenance.

## Required decision
For every candidate rule output one of:
`PROMOTE`, `RESTRICT`, `KEEP_AS_HYPOTHESIS`, `REJECT`, or `DEPRECATE`, with evidence and unresolved limitations.
