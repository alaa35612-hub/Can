# Black John Research Governance

## Mission
Use Black John knowledge as a criticizable research corpus and hypothesis library, never as an unquestionable decision tree.

## Authority order
1. Research protocol.
2. Raw observed market data.
3. Persistent State Ledgers.
4. Skills and pattern hypotheses.
5. Previous analyses and narrative outputs.

A lower layer may suggest questions but cannot override a higher layer. When a skill conflicts with causally valid data, the data wins and the conflict must be recorded for knowledge correction.

## Mandatory research posture
- Reconstruct facts before naming a pattern.
- Analyze every symbol chronologically across all available rows, files, batches and timeframes.
- Use closed candles and information available at the active cutoff only.
- Maintain multiple competing hypotheses, including an unidentified-structure hypothesis.
- Record supporting, opposing and missing evidence separately.
- Compare successful, failed and control cases.
- Separate structural importance, readiness, entry safety, confidence and data reliability.
- Treat previous conclusions as audit targets, not labels.
- Permit discovery of patterns absent from the current library.
- Abstain when evidence is insufficient or contradictory.

## Central instruction
Use Black John skills as background patterns, candidate explanations and research questions. Do not assume any rule, threshold or interpretation is correct. Rebuild the observable sequence from raw data, create competing hypotheses, search for evidence for and against each hypothesis, and test candidate rules on positive cases, failed cases, matched controls and blind replay. Maintain a causal State Ledger for every asset and timestamp every transition. Skills propose; data judges; protocol governs; blind replay tests; controls prevent illusion.

## Prohibitions
- No pattern-first fitting.
- No single-candle conclusions.
- No fixed universal analytical thresholds.
- No converting missing evidence to neutral evidence.
- No declaring a campaign terminated because a symbol disappeared from a ranked sample.
- No promotion of a rule from persuasive prose alone.
- No use of future outcome to justify a historical decision.

## Required output
Every conclusion must expose:
- observed facts;
- dominant and alternative hypotheses;
- evidence for, against and missing;
- temporal sequence;
- historical analogues and failed analogues;
- uncertainty and discriminating next evidence;
- invalidation conditions;
- research status of every invoked rule.
