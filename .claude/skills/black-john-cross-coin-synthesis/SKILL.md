# Black John Cross-Coin Research Synthesis

## Mission
Derive generalizable structural knowledge by comparing complete campaigns across symbols, outcomes, regimes and timeframes.

## Comparison unit
Compare campaigns, not isolated candles and not symbols by final return alone.

## Required campaign fields
- symbol, timeframe, regime and liquidity context;
- campaign birth, first warning, ignition, acceptance, peak, weakness and outcome times;
- dominant and alternative mechanisms;
- ordered price, OI, execution and positioning sequence;
- data reliability;
- entry freshness;
- success, failure or unresolved outcome;
- prior-analysis errors.

## Comparison axes
- leading variable and confirmation order;
- OI build, flush, reload, persistence or collapse;
- Trades/quote-volume shock and retention;
- price resilience, breakout, reclaim, acceptance and rejection;
- Global, Top Account and Top Position divergence;
- campaign age and distance from footprint;
- higher-timeframe support or conflict;
- successful versus failed analogues;
- early versus late detectability.

## Research method
1. Analyze each symbol independently against its own history.
2. Group campaigns by mechanism only after individual reconstruction.
3. Compare successful and failed cases within each group.
4. Identify necessary evidence, supporting evidence, discriminators and failure filters.
5. Search for counterexamples that invalidate broad claims.
6. Split mixed groups rather than forcing one taxonomy.
7. Create a new pattern family when repeated evidence is not explained by the existing library.

## Output
- campaign comparison matrix;
- pattern families and subtypes;
- common sequences;
- discriminators between success and failure;
- symbol-specific exceptions;
- corrected prior rules;
- new hypotheses;
- current-scan implications;
- unresolved research questions.

## Prohibition
Do not infer causality from the fact that all selected symbols were top gainers. Include negative and ordinary control windows to counter sample bias.
