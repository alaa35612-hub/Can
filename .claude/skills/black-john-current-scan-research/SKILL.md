# Black John Research-Grounded Current Scan

## Mission
Use only validated or explicitly conditional research knowledge to identify symbols currently building toward a possible upward campaign, without converting research hypotheses into automatic signals.

## Preconditions
- Current data must be quality-checked, chronologically merged and based on closed candles.
- The symbol's prior State Ledger must be loaded.
- Candidate rules must expose status and validation scope.
- Missing current observations must not erase prior campaign context.

## Scan procedure
1. Update each symbol's factual timeline and State Ledger.
2. Determine whether activity is a new campaign, continuation, reload, reset, failure or unresolved state.
3. Generate competing hypotheses before consulting pattern similarity.
4. Compare the current partial sequence with successful and failed historical analogues.
5. Identify which required evidence exists, which is missing and which contradicts the setup.
6. Evaluate freshness and distance from the original footprint.
7. State the next discriminating evidence required for promotion or invalidation.

## Separate outputs
For every symbol report independently:
- structural bias;
- signal importance;
- campaign readiness;
- entry safety;
- data reliability;
- research confidence;
- dominant and alternative hypotheses;
- evidence for, against and missing;
- current transition and campaign age;
- next discriminator and invalidation.

## Research buckets
- Early structural build;
- Confirmed build;
- Armed / near ignition;
- Live ignition;
- Continuation / reload;
- Cooling but structurally intact;
- Important but late / no chase;
- Failed / distribution;
- Unresolved / insufficient data.

## Ranking rule
Do not rank by one opaque score or by final-price similarity. Prefer symbols whose current ordered evidence matches validated early-stage sequences, remains close to the campaign footprint, has surviving fuel and execution confirmation, and differs materially from known failed analogues.

## Output standard
Explain why each selected symbol is preparing, why visually similar symbols were excluded, what evidence remains missing, and what must occur in the next update. Never claim certainty or guaranteed price movement.
