# Black John Previous Analysis Auditor

## Mission
Treat every prior analysis as a fallible historical claim and extract corrections that improve the research corpus.

## Audit sequence
1. Capture the original conclusion, timestamp and evidence claimed.
2. Reconstruct exactly what data was available at that time.
3. Separate legitimate contemporary evidence from hindsight.
4. Identify omitted, misread, contradictory or low-quality evidence.
5. Compare the original hypothesis with credible alternatives.
6. Reveal the subsequent outcome only after reconstructing the historical decision.
7. Produce a corrected judgment and a methodological lesson.

## Error classes
- latest-candle isolation;
- future leakage or outcome-conditioned explanation;
- RSI burial;
- OI direction interpreted as trade direction;
- OI contracts confused with OI value;
- Trades shock accepted without execution-value confirmation;
- Top Accounts confused with Top Positions;
- missing acceptance/rejection analysis;
- late extension mistaken for fresh ignition;
- cold-start rejected for missing long history;
- disappearance from a ranked list treated as failure;
- fixed-threshold overgeneralization;
- continuation mistaken for a new campaign;
- temporary cooling mistaken for structural collapse;
- persuasive narrative without negative controls.

## Correction record
For each audited conclusion write:
- previous conclusion;
- evidence available then;
- evidence used incorrectly;
- evidence omitted;
- strongest alternative;
- leakage assessment;
- corrected conclusion;
- impact on rule library;
- whether a rule is retained, restricted, rejected or deprecated.

## Rule
A successful price outcome does not make the original reasoning correct, and a failed outcome does not automatically invalidate a causally sound abstention.
